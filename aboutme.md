# Your experiences and academic performance, favorite classes etc. during college so far

I began my Chico experience attending the CS Summer Camp hosted by Professor Jaime Raigoza. I made many friends and connections through that, and it gave me a headstart to transfering into Chico.

I also enrolled in the UPE mentorship program, which I became accustomed to many of the upperclassmen of CS. I made many strong connections through that program.

- I currently have a 4.0 GPA

My favorite classes have been:

- 311: I'm an DS&A nerd and had fun, solving assignments like they were jigsaw puzzles
- Networks: I really enjoyed Kredo's lectures, the subject material (so cool to finally find out how things work "under the hood"). The assignments also felt like fun challenges.
- 301W: Really opened my mind to moral/ethical considerations associated with computers. I felt very welcomed in your class as well.
- Software Engineering (CSCI-430), makes me feel like I'm an actual software engineer in the real world. Dr. Buffardi is an excellent lecturer.
- Usability Engineering (CSCI-431W), I've always been a fan of user-centered design. It's so cool to understand how I could make my apps the best version for users.
