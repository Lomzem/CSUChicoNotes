# Tibetan Buddhism

"Vajrayana" Buddhism (the Diamond Vehicle)

Basic assumptions of Mahayana Buddhism (belief in bodhisattvas)

Chen Rezig (Tibetan translation of Avalokitesvara), bodhisattva of compassion

## Additional Beliefs
Bodhisattvas who reincarnate in lineages (**tulkus**)
- Dalai Lama is 14th incarnation of Avalokitesvara (Chen Rezig in Tibetan)
- Panchen Lama is incarnation of Amitabha
- Tenzin Gyatso is the 14th Dalai Lama
- Gedhun Choekyi Nyima is the 11th Panchen Lama

- Importance of guru (teacher, called **Lama** in Tibetan)
- Teachers revered in Buddhist schools (Lamaism)

# Tantra
- Set of religious practices found in both Hinduism and Buddhism passed from teacher to student
Involves:
- Visualizing deity (Hinduism) or buddha (Buddhism)
- Specific ritual practices/meditation
- Pursuit of moksha (Hinduism) or enlightenment (Buddhism)
    - Mudras
    - Mantra
    - Meditation

## Mudras, Mantra, Meditation
- Mantra is sacred chants
- Mudras are sacred gestures
    - Both oriented to specific bodhisattva
- Meditation involves visualizing enlightened being to take on their attributes or visualizing a mandala

## Left Hand Practices
- Some Tantra deliberately break social rules about monastic celibacy, alcohol, and vegetarianism to show moral rules are empty of inherent existence
- Includes ritual sexual activity (real or symbolic or sometimes spiritual w/dakini)
    - union of bliss and emptiness, or of wisdom and compassion

# Tibetan Politics
- Tibet borders China, Nepal, and India
- China views Tibet as its traditional homeland, invaded in 1959
- Dalai Lama (head of Gelugpa monastic order which ruled Tibet) fled into exile

# Tibetan Buddhism and Death
- Very important to prepare for death
- Death as possible stepping stone to enlightenment
- Acknowledge morality & prepare for it
- Tibetan term for body (lu) means "something you leave behind"

# Bardo State
- _Bardo Thodol_ is ritual text, explains what happens between death and rebirth
    - 49 day period w/strange encounters w/peaceful and wrathful bodhisattvas and projections of your own karmic accretions
- "Bardo" means in-between; state prior to reincarnation
- Tibetan death ritual is to help in bardo state

# Phowa Meditation
- Practice designed to prepare you for moment of death
- If properly prepared for death, could use bardo state to attain enlightenment

## Phowa Meditation Steps
1) Visualize bodhisattva (or deity)
2) Visualize sacred being sending out rays of light that purify all your negative karma
3) Visualize yourself as merging w/that sacred being

Amitabha Buddha is popular for phowa meditation
