# Daosim vs Confucianism

## Daoism
- Dao can't be described/understood in words
- Concerned w/nature/cosmos
- Humans can't change nature and shouldn't try
- Ideal behavior: follow nature (wu wei)
- Political leaders: should not strive, should follow water's way/way of nature

## Confucianism
- Way requires study of many written texts
- Concerned w/human society and relationships
- Education in part about changing society (always striving to be better)
- Ideal behavior: follow filial piety (xiao), rites (li), benevolence (ren)
- Political leaders should rule by strong moral example
