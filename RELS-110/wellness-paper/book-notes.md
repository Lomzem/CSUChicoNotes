- "Herbs are available from several sources. None of my informants in the countryside ever paid for herbs" (109)

- "Throughout tropical Southeast Asia, herbs and plant materials are widely used for their cooling qualities.8 In Vietnam, most green herbs are seen as cooling because of their moisture content (this was explained to me many times)" (112).

- cam nang: Heat invades the body (being affected by heat) (112)
- cam sot, bi nhiet: fever (112)

- Herbs used to cool and expel heat: san day (112)
- Fevers, whether the result of overheating or infection, can be lowered by binding herbs, including ma de or sai dat, to the hands and feet (112)

- "Expelling poisons (giai doc). Boils (mun nhot), suppurative skin condi- tions, ulcers (loet), and infected wounds have long been treated by apply- ing antibiotic poultices or liquid from several common herbs" (113)

- "The aim is to expel the toxic elements (normally, with these skin conditions, heat), and to cool the body" (113)

- "In most remedies, the liquid is applied directly to the affected areas" (113)

- "Headaches are relieved with wind-beating massages as well as by placing or binding herbs which may have been dried and heated first—against the forehead, and by drinking certain herb juices" (114)

- Pain, including rheumatoid pain, and swelling in other parts of the body (hands, feet, back, gums) is relieved by placing herbal poultices (ngai cuu, cuc tan, nho noi, la lot) against the affected ar.ea or by drinking a decoction (114)
