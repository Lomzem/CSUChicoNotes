## Topics to Cover
- Define sangha: Buddhist monks/nuns who live communally and follow a collective set of rules: Vinaya
- Sangha is found in both Therevada and Mahayana traditions
    - Therevada: seen as main path to becoming an arhat, one who exits the cycle of samsara to attain nirvana
- In Therevada countries, sangha and laypeople are interdependent
    - Sangha depend on laypeople for offerings
    - Laypeople depend on sangha for spiritual guidance
