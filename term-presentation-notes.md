# Summary
- Shorter attention span
- Shallow processing of information
- Reduction of critical thinking

# Critical Thinking
- Carr gave lots of evidence for his claims
- Carr gave personal and relatable anecdotes
- Carr gave accounts from Google designers about how websites are designed to move fast
- Carr oversimplified: books good, computers bad
- People could use own discretion
- 2015 Study, MS Office students wrote vs pen-and-paper writers in English Second Language students
    - At end, both given pen paper, MS Office had better English mechanics and higher quality paragraphs

# Comparative Analysis
- Carr is right about attention
- 2015 study concluded that Internet usage affects attention span, and it is getting shorter
- Meta-analysis of 30 studies from 2024 found that computer-support-collaborative-learning was a good way to **improve** critical thinking
- Study from 2024 compared direct strategy with computer-assisted strategy teaching
- CAST scored a better score on Reading Comprehension Test
